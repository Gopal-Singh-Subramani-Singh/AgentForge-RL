Implement a Python BankAccount class in solution.py.

Requirements:

1. Constructor:
   BankAccount(owner, initial_balance=0)

2. Properties:
   - owner
   - balance
   - transaction_history

3. deposit(amount):
   - Adds amount to balance.
   - Amount must be greater than 0.
   - Otherwise raise ValueError.
   - Record the transaction.

4. withdraw(amount):
   - Subtracts amount from balance.
   - Amount must be greater than 0.
   - Cannot withdraw more than the available balance.
   - Invalid amounts raise ValueError.
   - Insufficient funds raise ValueError.
   - Record successful transactions.

5. transfer(other_account, amount):
   - Transfers money to another BankAccount.
   - Amount must be greater than 0.
   - Cannot transfer more than the available balance.
   - Update both accounts.
   - Record transactions for both accounts.

6. transaction_history:
   - Must be a list.
   - Successful deposits, withdrawals, incoming transfers,
     and outgoing transfers must be recorded.
   - Failed operations must not be recorded.
